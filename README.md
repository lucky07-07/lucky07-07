## 🧠 About Me

I'm an AI/ML engineer and Master's student in Data Science at **Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Bengaluru**, building deep-learning systems that hold up to both research scrutiny and production load. I constructed the **first large-scale Kannada image-caption dataset** (250K captions over 50K MS COCO images) and benchmarked CNN-RNN against Transformer architectures for a paper currently under review.

My work also spans **carbon-aware autonomous agents for Kubernetes** (LangGraph + a local Llama model for explainable scheduling) and **real-time geospatial intelligence** streaming satellites, aircraft, and weather through Kafka, PyTorch, and Spark. On the vision side I push **imbalance-aware fine-grained classification**, reaching **97.48% accuracy** with a transformer ensemble on rice-leaf disease recognition. I care about the full path — dataset construction, training, ensembling, explainability — and shipping models behind real **FastAPI services and React dashboards**.

## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🔗 [KIC-COCO — Kannada Image Captioning](https://github.com/lucky07-07/KIC-COCO-Kannada-Image-Captioning)
> The first large-scale, standardized Kannada image-caption dataset (250K captions over 50K MS COCO images via IndicTrans2-1B), with a benchmark of six CNN-RNN and Transformer encoder-decoder architectures and back-translation semantic verification.

`Python` `PyTorch` `IndicTrans2` `Transformers` `ConvNeXt / ViT / Swin / CLIP` `SacreBLEU` `BERTScore`

📊 **Key result** — ConvNeXtTransformer: SacreBLEU-4 **68.65**, chrF **43.20**, back-translation **BERTScore 84.02** — vs. 7.26 BLEU-4 for prior Kannada captioning work. *(Paper under review.)*

---

### 🔗 [SustainSched — Carbon-Aware Kubernetes Orchestration](https://github.com/lucky07-07/autonomous-carbon-k8s-scheduler)
> An autonomous AI agent that schedules Kubernetes workloads across 14 UK grid regions by carbon intensity, combining offline forecasting with a local Llama 3.2 model for explainable, audited scheduling decisions.

`Python` `LangGraph` `Ollama / Llama 3.2` `XGBoost` `PyTorch` `FastAPI` `Kubernetes` `Prometheus` `Grafana`

📊 **Key result** — XGBoost forecaster wins on a held-out set with **RMSE 9.29, MAE 7.16, R² 0.917**, driving carbon-aware decisions across 14 regions with a full Prometheus + Grafana command center.

---

### 🔗 [Geospatial Monitoring System](https://github.com/lucky07-07/geospatial-monitoring-system)
> A real-time multi-domain geospatial intelligence platform that tracks satellites, aircraft, and weather from live public sources, streams them through Kafka into PostGIS, and serves a mode-switched React + CesiumJS dashboard with a PySpark analytics layer.

`Python` `Apache Kafka` `FastAPI` `PostGIS` `PyTorch (STGCN/LSTM)` `PySpark` `React` `CesiumJS` `Docker`

📊 **Key result** — Live validation tracking **126 satellites + 1,512 aircraft**, publishing 100 satellite (90-min) and 459 aircraft (30-min) forecasts per cycle across 8 containerized microservices.

---

### 🔗 [AgroVision-7 — Rice Leaf Disease Classification](https://github.com/lucky07-07/rice-leaf-disease-classification)
> A lightweight deep-learning study for fine-grained recognition of seven rice leaf conditions, progressing from a weak imbalanced baseline to an Optuna-optimized, test-time-augmented transformer ensemble.

`Python` `PyTorch` `ViT-B/16` `Swin-T` `ConvNeXt` `Optuna` `Focal Loss` `Grad-CAM`

📊 **Key result** — ViT + Swin-T + ConvNeXt ensemble (TTA×5): **97.48% accuracy / 0.9750 macro-F1**, a +17.2-point lift over the non-augmented baseline, with every per-class accuracy ≥ 0.96.

---

### 🔗 [Sentient — Self-Verifying AI Intelligence Terminal](https://github.com/lucky07-07/sentient)
> A self-running intelligence terminal that monitors the AI ecosystem across eight sources and turns the noise into a grounded daily briefing through a closed draft → verify → revise → publish agent loop that rewrites its own constitution over time.

`TypeScript` `Next.js 14` `Groq (qwen3-32b)` `Gemini Embeddings` `LanceDB` `D3.js`

📊 **Key result** — Agentic RAG pipeline over 8 live sources with a critic pass that verifies every claim against source chunks before publishing, plus a self-improving meta-agent.
